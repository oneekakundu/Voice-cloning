Elephants are large mammals of which two species are traditionally recognized, the African  elephant and the Asian elephant.  Elephants are scattered throughout Sub-Saharan Africa, South Asia and Southeast Asia.  Male African elephants are the largest extent, terrestrial animals and can reach a height of 13  feet and weight 7,000 kg.  All elephants have several distinctive features and most notable of which is a long trunk.  Used for many purposes, particularly breathing, lifting, water and grasping objects.
